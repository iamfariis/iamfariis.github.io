When a purchase is available

If a product is made available for purchase, the checkout page and applicable payment provider or merchant-of-record will present the purchase terms, taxes, delivery information, and any applicable refund or cancellation process before payment.

Digital products

Because digital products may be delivered or made accessible electronically, refund eligibility can depend on the product, delivery status, the terms shown at checkout, and applicable consumer-protection rights. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Requesting help

For a purchase-related question or refund request, contact team@opnexta.com with the product name, order details if available, and the reason for your request. We will review the request and respond using the applicable purchase terms and law.

Changes

We may update this policy when our products, checkout process, or practices change. The current version will be posted on this page.
