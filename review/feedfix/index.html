FeedFix

A digital software tool for identifying catalog and product-feed changes that can create downstream shopping-channel issues.

Demonstration only: This public sample uses synthetic data. No login, private account access, customer data, or live checkout is involved.

Sample input

Platform / channel
Shopify → Google

Issue class
Products are missing or not visible

What FeedFix checks

A deterministic first-pass workflow covering product, variant, price, availability, identifier, and market-visibility issue categories.

Structured diagnostic output

Priority: Review the selected channel's product-feed visibility path first.

Check first: Confirm that the affected product is eligible and visible for the selected channel and market.

Check second: Compare product, variant, availability, and identifier information between the catalog and downstream feed.

Do not change yet: Do not alter catalog data until the source of the visibility issue has been isolated.

What the customer receives

FeedFix is delivered electronically as a digital diagnostic product with usage instructions. No physical shipment or consulting service is involved.
